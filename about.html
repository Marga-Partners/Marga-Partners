About

Most advisors know how a product gets built, or how it gets sold. Our founder has led both.

Pharmacist and scientist by training. Thirteen years running clinical development, upstream commercialization, launch, and brand and portfolio strategy at three of the manufacturers that define the category.

Why Marga exists

The path is the practice.

Def.
mārga (n.)
/ mahr-guh /

Sanskrit. The path, the way, the route. A disciplined method for navigating complexity toward a destination.

The route from scientific innovation to commercial success is never a straight line. It is a chain of connected decisions across discovery, development, regulation, launch, lifecycle and adoption. Too many organizations optimize one link and lose the chain.

Marga is founder led and supported by an expert network. You work with the person whose judgment you came for, and you are told plainly which parts of a question the evidence can and cannot answer.
