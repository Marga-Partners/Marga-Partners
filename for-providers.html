The Marga Exchange

A working community, not a mailing list.

A working network of injectors, dermatologists, plastic surgeons and practice owners whose judgment informs Marga Intelligence. Members contribute what they see in the chair, and get back the view no single practice can assemble alone.

Open to anyone who treats patients or runs a practice. Participation is voluntary, and what you get back scales with what the panel puts in.

What membership gives you

Four things you cannot get on your own.

  • 01

    Benchmarking

    See your pricing, product mix, retention and per-patient economics against comparable practices. Anonymized, normalized, and sharper as the panel grows.

  • 02

    Bespoke market research

    Opportunities to participate in commissioned studies for manufacturers, investors and boards. Your read on a product reaches the people making the decision.

  • 03

    Exclusive events

    Small closed-door sessions and dinners with peers who run practices at your level, plus the occasional manufacturer in the room answering real questions.

  • 04

    Category intelligence

    Member briefings on what is coming, what is being oversold, and what the data actually supports, before it reaches a conference stage.

How it works

Light commitment. Real signal.

  • 01

    Join

    A short form: who you are, where you practise and what you do. No cost, no committee.

  • 02

    Contribute when it suits you

    The benchmark survey opens as soon as you join, then one short survey a quarter. Deeper studies are always optional.

  • 03

    Receive the benchmark

    Your position against the panel, plus the member briefing each cycle.

  • 04

    Come to the table

    Invitations to closed sessions and to research the industry is actually paying for.
